What Happens in Each Mode?

🔴 Mode 1 - Debugger Trap:

When you try to modify content, the debugger will trigger repeatedly (every 100ms). This makes it extremely annoying and nearly impossible to edit content, as you'll be stuck in debugger pause mode.

🟡 Mode 2 - Visible Labels:

Any modified content immediately gets a bright yellow "[MODIFIED]" label that blinks. This makes tampered content obvious in screenshots and prevents fraudulent use.

Combines both modes. Content gets labeled AND debugger trap activates. This is the maximum deterrent against tampering. Warning: Very aggressive!
